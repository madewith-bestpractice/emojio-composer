EMOJIO + MIDI

Play emoji music from a real keyboard

Plug in a MIDI keyboard, drum pads or a controller. Every key plays the emoji you picked, at the note you press. Hit record and your playing lands on the staff as stickers, ready to loop, save and share.

MIDI is part of Premium, a one-time purchase with no subscription. The app is free to download. No keyboard handy? Try Emojio in your browser (the web version has no MIDI).

What it does

🎹

Every key is an emoji

Pick a frog, a bell or a drum, and the keyboard plays that sound at the pitch you press. Listen on all channels or just one, and shift octaves up or down.

🔴

Record into the staff

Turn on recording, press Play, and play along. Each note drops onto the staff at the playhead, on the right line, so you can see and edit what you played.

💥

Soft or loud

Velocity sets each note's loudness, and it's saved with the note. On a tablet, stylus or Apple Pencil pressure does the same when you tap stickers in.

🥁

Drum pads play your palette

Map pads or keys to the stickers in your palette. A pad learns the slot, so whatever emoji you put there is what it plays.

🎛️

Knobs and buttons, learned

Play/Stop, Clear, next and previous voice, octave, and a jog wheel to scrub through the song. Each comes with a default, and MIDI learn fits your controller.

⏱️

In time with your gear

Send your song's notes out on any channel to drive a synth or drum machine. Follow another device's MIDI clock, or (on iPhone, iPad and Mac) send clock so your gear follows Emojio.

How to start

  1. Connect. Plug in a USB MIDI keyboard or controller (a USB-C adapter works for most keyboards). On iPhone, iPad and Mac you can also scan for Bluetooth MIDI.
  2. Open the 🎹 MIDI sheet in Emojio and tap Connect next to your device.
  3. Pick an emoji from your palette and play. Switch emoji as you go to layer a melody, a bass line and drums.
  4. Record and save. Turn on "Record into grid while playing", press Play, and play along with the loop. Save the song, export it as audio or video, or share a link.

Questions

Do I need to read music?

No. If you can press a key, you can play. Recorded notes show up as emoji on the staff, and you can edit them like any sticker you tapped in.

Is MIDI included with the app?

MIDI is part of Premium. Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and it also unlocks every sticker sound, video export and unlimited saved songs.

Which keyboards work?

Any class-compliant USB MIDI keyboard, pad controller or synth, which covers most of them. On iPhone, iPad and Mac, Bluetooth MIDI devices work too.
