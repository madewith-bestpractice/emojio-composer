# Store text for the free + Premium release (1.1.0)

Drafted 2026-10-05 from the live listings. Apply when the build ships, not
before: the Play listing goes live the moment its edit is committed, and the
App Store text belongs to the new version.

Guideline 2.3.2 requires the description to mark which features need a
purchase, so Premium items say so.

## iOS description (en-US)

MAKE MUSIC OUT OF EMOJI — FREE.

Emojio turns your iPad or iPhone into a playful music studio where every emoji is an instrument. Tap a frog, a dog, a drum, a bell — drop the stickers onto the staff and watch your little song come to life. No notes to read, no theory to learn. If you can pick an emoji, you can write a tune.

PAINT A SONG
• A big, friendly staff — tap to drop an emoji, tap it again to remove it.
• Every emoji has its own voice. Faces, animals, music and a full drum kit are free; Premium adds vehicles, characters, food, sports, nature and more.
• Set the pace with the turtle-and-hare tempo slider, hit Play, and hear it loop.
• Apple Pencil pressure sets each note's volume, so you can play it soft or loud (Premium).

SAVE AND SHARE
• Save your songs right on your device — name them and reopen anytime. Free keeps 3; Premium keeps as many as you like.
• Export your song as an audio file (WAV), or as a video (MP4) to post anywhere (Premium).
• Share a link that opens your song right back up in Emojio.

PLAY WITH REAL GEAR (PREMIUM)
• Connect a MIDI keyboard — USB or Bluetooth — to play and record live.
• Send notes and MIDI clock out to drive external gear, or follow an external clock to stay in sync.

Studio-quality sound that plays even with the silent switch on.

MADE TO BE PLAYED WITH
Emojio is for kids, families, beginners, and anyone who ever wanted to make music but felt locked out by staffs and sheet music. It's silly, it's colorful, and it sounds surprisingly good.

FREE, WITH AN OPTIONAL PREMIUM
Emojio is free to play, with no time limit. New players get Premium free for their first 3 days. After that, Premium is a single one-time purchase — no subscription, ever. Already bought the Lifetime Unlock? You have Premium.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Emojio collects nothing. No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no accounts. Your songs live on your device and only leave when you share them yourself.

## App Review note

Emojio is now free to use. Premium is an optional one-time, non-consumable purchase (emojio.unlock_forever, the same product as before). To find it: tap the ⭐ Premium button in the toolbar, or tap any 🔒 sticker in the + sticker picker (e.g. the 🚗 tab), the 🔒 Video (MP4) option under Export, or the 🔒 MIDI button. Restore Purchase is on the Premium sheet. On a fresh install Premium is free for 3 days, so locks won't show until the trial ends; the ⭐ Premium button is always visible.
